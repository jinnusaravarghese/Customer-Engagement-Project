# Customer Engagement & Product Utilization Analytics for Retention Strategy

* **Domain:** Financial Analyst Intern Project (Unified Mentor)
* **Objective:** Analyze customer engagement, product utilization to improve customer retention in the banking sector.

## Dataset Overview
* **Total Records:** 5,000 Customers
* **Features:** 13 Variables (including Customer ID, Surname, Credit Score, Geography, Gender, Age, Tenure, Balance, Num of Products, Has Cr Card, Is Active Member, Estimated Salary, and Exited)

## **Tools Used**
* Microsoft Excel, Python, Visual Studio Code, and Streamlit

## **Analysis**
* Customer engagement analysis
* Product utilization analysis
* Key Performance Indicators (KPI) Ccalculations
* Churn Analysis

## **Findings**
   * Active customers and customers using multiple banking products showed higher retention, 
    while inactive high-balance customers had a higher risk of churn

## **Recommendations**
     * Improve customer engagement, promote multi- product adoption , strength loyalty

  * **Outcome**
   * Developed an inactive dashboard to visualize customer engagement,
    product utilization, and churn insights
